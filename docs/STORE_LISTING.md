## Full description (4000 chars max — works for both stores)

Lumen is a beautiful, reverent home for the Catholic Bible — written in simple, modern English so every verse is easy to read and easy to pray with.

All 73 books of the Catholic canon, including the Deuterocanon (Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch, 1 & 2 Maccabees, and the Greek portions of Esther and Daniel) are included from the start.

WHAT'S INSIDE

• The complete Catholic Bible — Old & New Testament, all 73 books
• Daily Mass readings updated every day
• A clean, distraction-free reader with adjustable text
• Bookmarks and highlights that sync across your devices
• Powerful verse search across the whole Bible
• Reading plans to guide you through Scripture
• Designed offline-first — read anywhere, no signal needed
• Light & dark themes, optimized for evening reading

DESIGNED FOR PRAYER

Lumen is built to feel sacred. Warm parchment tones, an elegant serif typeface, generous breathing room — every detail is shaped for quiet reading and reflection. No ads. No noise.
