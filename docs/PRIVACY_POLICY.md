# Privacy Policy — Lumen

_Last updated: May 7, 2026_

Lumen ("we", "our", "the app") is operated by the Lumen team. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

## 1. Information We Collect

**Account information.** When you create an account we collect your email address and a hashed password. Optional: display name.

**Usage data.** We collect anonymous, aggregate analytics (page views, feature usage) via Plausible Analytics. Plausible does not use cookies and does not collect personal data. See https://plausible.io/data-policy.

**Content you create.** Bookmarks, highlights, and reading progress you save in the app are stored against your account so you can access them across devices.

**Subscription data.** If you subscribe to Lumen Premium, the App Store (Apple) or Google Play handles your payment. We receive only the subscription status (active / canceled / expiry) — never your payment details.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

- To provide and maintain the app (sign-in, sync, bookmarks, preferences)
- To process subscriptions and unlock premium features
- To improve the app via aggregate analytics
- To respond to support requests
- To comply with legal obligations

## 3. How We Share Your Information

We do **not** sell your personal data. We share data only with:

- **Service providers** that help us run the app (hosting, authentication, analytics).
- **Payment platforms** (Apple App Store, Google Play) for subscription processing.
- **Legal authorities** when required by law.

## 4. Data Retention

We retain your account and content while your account is active. You may delete your account at any time from in-app Settings; we will delete your personal data within 30 days, except where law requires longer retention (e.g. tax records).

## 5. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (GDPR, CCPA, Kenya Data Protection Act, etc.) you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data
- Delete your account and data
- Export your data
- Opt out of analytics

To exercise any right, email **privacy@lumencatholic.com**.

## 6. Children

Lumen is intended for users aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, contact us and we will delete it.

## 7. Security

We use industry-standard safeguards (TLS in transit, encryption at rest via our cloud provider, hashed passwords, row-level security on the database). No system is 100% secure, but we work hard to protect your data.

## 8. International Transfers

Lumen is operated from Kenya and our cloud infrastructure is hosted in the European Union and United States. By using Lumen you consent to your data being processed in those jurisdictions.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be announced in-app or by email. Continued use of Lumen after changes constitutes acceptance.
